02 · Materials Research · Mar–Jun 202402 · 材料研究 · 2024.03–06

Electronic Structure of In-doped GaAs: First-principles Analysis半导体材料电子结构研究：In掺杂GaAs第一性原理分析

Applied density functional theory in Materials Studio to model In-doped GaAs and compare how different doping configurations affect band structure, density of states and optical properties. The analysis found an approximately 40% reduction in band gap and a clear red shift in the optical absorption edge, helping explain the mechanism through which doping changes semiconductor optoelectronic performance.基于密度泛函理论（DFT），使用Materials Studio构建In掺杂GaAs晶胞模型，对比不同掺杂方式对能带结构、电子态密度及光学性质的影响。研究发现掺杂后材料禁带宽度下降约40%，光吸收边出现明显红移，揭示掺杂方式影响半导体材料光电性能的作用机制。
