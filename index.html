About关于我

I studied Physics at Shandong University with a minor in Finance and am currently pursuing an MSc in Investment at the University of Birmingham. This training gives me an interdisciplinary foundation across physics, finance and data analytics. During my undergraduate and postgraduate studies, I have worked on financial-market forecasting, asset pricing, corporate valuation and industry research, using machine-learning methods for financial data modelling. One of these projects received a university-level first prize.

Through internships and project work in private equity, securities research, commercial banking and customer analytics, I have contributed to industry research, financial and valuation analysis, due diligence, meeting documentation and broader research support. I value accuracy, responsibility and dependable execution, and am comfortable working in demanding, fast-paced research environments.

I maintain a sustained interest in technology, AI, geopolitics, capital markets and emerging industries. I also enjoy turning ideas into working products, including Suntree, a live interactive-fiction platform. Curious by nature, I learn new methods quickly and put them into practice. I hope to deepen my capabilities in securities and investment research, producing rigorous and timely work that supports team decisions and client service.
